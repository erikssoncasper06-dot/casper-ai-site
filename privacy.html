Privacy Policy — Casper AI

Casper AI is a private, personal application used by a single individual (its owner). It is not distributed to the public.

What data is processed

With the owner's explicit consent via their bank (PSD2/open banking), the app reads the owner's own account balances and transactions for personal budgeting purposes. Product searches query public retail sources. Portfolio prices come from public market data.

Storage & security

All data is stored on a private server controlled by the owner, protected by TLS and authenticated sessions. Bank credentials are never seen or stored by the app; bank consent is given directly at the bank (e.g. BankID). No payments can be initiated — read-only access (AIS) only.

Sharing

Data is never sold or shared with third parties.

Deletion & contact

The owner can disconnect the bank connection and delete all data at any time. Contact for data protection matters: erikssoncasper06@gmail.com.
